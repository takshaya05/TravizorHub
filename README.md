# TravizorHub – Your Gateway to Smooth Journeys

## Problem Statement
Travellers often face difficulty understanding foreign country laws, travel regulations, visa guidelines, and legal restrictions due to complex legal language and scattered information across multiple official sources. There is a need for a platform that simplifies legal knowledge for travellers and provides **real-time guidance** in an easy-to-understand format.

## Proposed Solution
**TravizorHub** is an **AI-powered travel legal guidance platform** that helps users understand international travel rules through:

- **AI & NLP Processing** – Simplifies and summarizes complex legal documents  
- **Country Law Comparison Engine** – Compares regulations between home and destination countries  
- **Real-Time AI Chatbot** – Provides instant travel legal assistance  
- **Interactive Dashboard** – Displays travel inputs, legal comparisons, and guidance summaries  

## Architecture
1. **User Authentication Module** – Register and login system  
2. **Traveller Input Layer** – Accepts home country, destination country, and legal query  
3. **Legal Data Aggregation Module** – Collects data from government, embassy, and travel advisory sources  
4. **AI Processing Layer** – Uses NLP models to simplify, translate, and summarize legal texts  
5. **Comparison Analysis Engine** – Highlights legal differences and permissions  
6. **Chatbot Assistance Layer** – Provides conversational guidance  
7. **Dashboard Visualization Module** – Displays structured results

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS, HTML, CSS, JavaScript  
- **Backend:** Node.js / Express.js  
- **Database:** MongoDB  
- **AI/NLP Models:** Ollama / Open-source LLMs  
- **Authentication:** JWT-based secure login  
- **Deployment:** AWS / Vercel

## Features

### Traveller Input System
- Home country and destination country selection  
- Legal query text input  
- Smart validation of travel data  

### Legal Data Processing
- Fetch travel legal documents from trusted advisory sources  
- Clean, preprocess, and structure legal text  
- Summarize and translate complex regulations  

### Law Comparison Engine
- Side-by-side country law comparison  
- Highlight restrictions, permissions, and differences  
- Provide short explanation summary below comparison table  

### AI Chatbot Assistance
- Real-time travel legal guidance  
- Context-aware conversation support  
- Interactive chat window inside dashboard
